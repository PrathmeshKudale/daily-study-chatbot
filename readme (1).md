# Daily Study Chatbot 📚🤖

A friendly, interactive AI study coach built with Streamlit and powered by the Google Gemini API. Designed specifically for students from Class 1 to 12 to build custom study plans, take quick quizzes, and maintain study streaks!

## Features
- **Customized Learning Context:** Set your Class (1–12), favorite/current subject, and available study time.
- **AI Study Planner:** Automatically generates a personalized timetable with breaks.
- **Interactive Quizzes:** Generates quick 3-question quizzes on your chosen topic.
- **Streak Tracker:** Keep your study momentum alive with streak counters and daily completion markers.
- **Streaming AI Responses:** Real-time chat streaming for an engaging experience.
